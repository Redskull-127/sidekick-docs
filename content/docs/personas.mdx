A sidekick is a small record: a name, a glyph, a color, an Apple voice, a catchphrase, and a character brief of 60 to 120 words. The brief becomes a section of the system prompt while the sidekick is active, so Claude speaks as that character while keeping every ability it has.

## The two that ship

| | Voice | Character |
| :- | :- | :- |
| 🧭 **Ada** | Ava | Calm, methodical staff engineer. Reads before writing, names the root cause before the fix. |
| 🦊 **Rudy** | Tom | Blunt senior developer paged at 3am too often. Prefers deleting code to adding it. Dry, never cruel. |

## Making your own

```text
/sidekick new <describe someone>
```

The description goes to Claude (Haiku) through your own account, and the result is checked: the voice has to be one of the [sixteen natural voices](/docs/voices), the glyph one emoji, the color one of six. Anything else falls back to a default. Good descriptions name an attitude and a habit:

- `a patient teacher who explains every step and never assumes`
- `Mira, a security reviewer who distrusts every input and says so`
- `an upbeat release engineer who ships small and celebrates green builds`

## What every persona is told

Whatever the character, the same rules ride along:

- Speak in first person as the persona; keep every Claude Code ability.
- **Open every reply with one plain sentence** stating the outcome. That sentence is what gets spoken.
- **Keep it short**: the opening sentence, then at most five short lines. Expand only when asked.
- **Ask, don't guess**: when input or a decision is needed, ask at once with the question tool, one question, short concrete options.
- "Brief?" or "what did you do?" gets two to four lines. A fix says what was broken and what changed.
- In talk mode: the whole reply is one to three spoken sentences under sixty words; code and detail go below a `---` line, shown but not read.

## Switching and removing

```text
/sidekick use Ada      switch
/sidekick list         everyone, the active one marked
/sidekick voice Ava    change the active sidekick's voice (see Voices)
/sidekick rm Nova      remove
/sidekick off          plain Claude again
```

Or open the pane with `/sidekick` and press `1`–`9`.

## Where they live

In Claude Code's plugin store, `~/.claude/plugins/store/`, as plain JSON. The active sidekick and your mute setting persist across sessions; talk mode does not.
