---
title: Quickstart
description: A two-minute conversation with a sidekick you made.
icon: Zap
---

<Steps>
<Step>
### Create someone
```text
/sidekick new Nova, an upbeat release engineer who ships small and explains briefly
```
Haiku turns the sentence into a persona: name, glyph, color, voice, catchphrase, and a short character brief. Nova is active at once.
</Step>
<Step>
### Start talking
```text
/sidekick talk
```
One chime. Say what you want done, then pause. Your words appear in the band as they are recognized, then they are sent as your prompt.
</Step>
<Step>
### Hear the answer, and cut in
Nova answers in a sentence or two, out loud. Another chime means it's your turn. If the answer is going the wrong way, just talk over it: two words that aren't Nova's own stop the speech and become your next prompt.
</Step>
<Step>
### Decide by voice
When Nova needs a choice, the question and its numbered options are read aloud. Say "the second one", say the option's name, say "yes", or answer in a few words of your own.
</Step>
<Step>
### Stop
Say **"stop"** while Nova is working to cancel the turn. Say **"end talk"**, or press `x` on the band, to leave talk mode. A lower chime confirms.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Useful next

- `/sidekick` opens the roster pane: `1`–`9` switch sidekicks, `m` mutes, `t` toggles talk, `0` turns the sidekick off.
- `/sidekick mute` keeps the persona and header but stops the speech.
- `/sidekick off` returns to plain Claude.
