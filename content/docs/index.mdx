Sidekick is a [Claude Code mod](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/plugins/mods/overview). It hands your session to a persona you describe in one sentence, and that persona is the one talking to you: it explains, does and fixes things in character, asks you the moment it needs a decision, and speaks its replies in its own voice. In talk mode you talk back, hands-free.

```text
/sidekick new Rudy, a blunt senior Rust dev who hates abstractions
  🦊 Rudy is ready. "Ship it or delete it."

/sidekick talk
  🎙 Talk mode on. Just speak; Rudy answers out loud and listens again.
```

## What changes

- **Every reply is headed by the sidekick**: its glyph and name, the spinner says `Rudy is on it…`, the question dialog says `Rudy asks:`.
- **It asks instead of guessing.** The persona is told to use Claude Code's question tool the moment input is needed, with short, concrete options.
- **It is short.** The first sentence of a reply is spoken; the whole reply is a few lines unless you ask for more.
- **You can talk to it.** Talk mode listens, sends what you said, speaks the answer, and listens again. Talk over it to interrupt.
